# Three-minute demo storyboard

## 0:00–0:25 — The decision

Screen: one sentence, forecast cutoff, target quarter, and three candidate assumptions. Say: “Using only information available by this date, what should a planner have expected, and what should change after seeing the miss?”

## 0:25–0:55 — Evidence boundary

Screen: compact timeline of publication dates and target quarter. Highlight that later recasts are stored separately. Show one definition warning. Do not lead with tooling.

## 0:55–1:30 — Forecast versus baselines

Screen: holdout table with driver model, prior-quarter baseline, seasonal baseline, actual, and errors. Show all quarters. State if the driver model loses. Avoid “accuracy” without naming metric and sample.

## 1:30–2:20 — Explain the featured miss

Screen: waterfall from forecast to actual. Reveal volume effect, effective-yield effect, and any residual. State bridge order. Say that effective yield captures mix and non-volume mechanics and is not a pure fee-rate estimate.

## 2:20–2:45 — Management implication

Screen: one decision card: retain, revise, or investigate. Recommended language: “Do not infer a pricing change from the public data; investigate the mix behind effective yield before changing the planning assumption.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Strongest objection

Screen: sample-size and comparability limitation plus exact evidence request. End with: “Which evidence would you require before treating the yield movement as controllable?”

## Demo failure conditions

Restart the recording if the demo hides a losing holdout, calls correlation causal, mixes original and recast vintages, implies internal Coinbase knowledge, or exceeds 3:15.
