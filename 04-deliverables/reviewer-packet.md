# Reviewer packet specification

The eventual packet is two pages maximum and requires no account, wallet, workbook software, or live dashboard.

## Page 1

- Decision question and information cutoff.
- One forecast-versus-actual chart including naive baseline.
- One exact variance waterfall.
- Recommendation and strongest objection.

## Page 2

- Four-line method: sample, holdouts, models, metrics.
- Evidence labels: reported, calculated, assumed.
- Definition-vintage note and excluded periods.
- Links/QR codes to memo, workbook, data, SQL, and validation report.
- Critique question: “What additional evidence would you require before changing the effective-yield assumption?”

## Reviewer checklist

1. Can you identify what was knowable at the cutoff?
2. Does the bridge reconcile?
3. Is any calculated proxy presented as a reported metric or causal driver?
4. Is the recommendation actionable without claiming private knowledge?
5. Which single assumption should be challenged first?

The feedback log records reviewer role, date, artifact version, response, resulting change, and remaining disagreement. No endorsement is inferred.
