# Coinbase Strategic Finance descriptive revenue bridge

**DESCRIPTIVE / NOT A FORECAST**  
**Comparison:** 2024-09-30 to 2024-12-31

## Result

Consumer transaction revenue changed by **$863.8 million**.
The fixed-order algebraic bridge attributes **$852.9 million**
to volume and **$10.9 million** to calculated effective yield,
with a **$0.0 million** residual.

## Interpretation boundary

This decomposition is descriptive. It does not establish that volume or
effective yield caused the revenue change. Effective yield is a calculated
proxy, not a reported causal driver. The bridge is descriptive, the calculated yield is not a reported fee rate, and the series uses a controlled reporting-definition vintage.
