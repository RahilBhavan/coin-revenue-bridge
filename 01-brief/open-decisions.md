## Scope-cut order

When time or evidence is short, cut in this order: interactive dashboard → macro driver extension → secondary revenue line → formatted PDF automation → extra holdouts. Never cut source provenance, vintage controls, baseline comparison, bridge reconciliation, or the memo.
